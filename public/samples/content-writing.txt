Rights. Writer may not republish or excerpt the article anywhere.

Cancellation. Publisher may cancel any assignment at any time. If Publisher cancels an assignment, no fee is owed.

Warranty and Indemnity. Writer represents that each article is original and does not infringe or defame. Writer will indemnify Publisher against any claim arising from an article, including claims arising from edits made by Publisher.

Disputes. This agreement is governed by New York law. Any dispute must be brought in the state or federal courts in New York County, New York.
