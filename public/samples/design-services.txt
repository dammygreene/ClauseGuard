DESIGN SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Design Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made on June 9, 2026 between Harbor & Pine Coffee Co., an Oregon corporation ("Client"), and Luis Moreno, doing business as Moreno Studio ("Designer").

1. Project. Designer will create a brand identity for Client consisting of a primary logo, a secondary mark, a color palette, a typography system, and a two-page brand guidelines document (the "Deliverables"), as further described in the attached Proposal dated June 2, 2026. The project includes up to two (2) rounds of revisions per Deliverable. Additional revisions will be billed at $95 per hour.

2. Fees. The total project fee is $6,800. Client will pay a non-refundable deposit of 50% ($3,400) before work begins, and the remaining 50% upon delivery of the final files. Invoices are due within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month.

3. Timeline. Designer will deliver initial concepts within three (3) weeks of receiving the deposit. Delays caused by Client's late feedback will extend the timeline accordingly.

4. Ownership. Upon Designer's receipt of full payment, Designer assigns to Client all rights, title, and interest in the final, approved Deliverables. Designer retains ownership of all preliminary concepts, sketches, and unused designs, and of any pre-existing tools, templates, or fonts, which are licensed to Client only as needed to use the Deliverables. Designer may display the Deliverables in Designer's portfolio after Client's public launch.

5. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement with fourteen (14) days' written notice. If Client terminates before completion, Client will pay for all work performed through the termination date at Designer's hourly rate, and in any case not less than the deposit. Ownership of any partially completed Deliverables transfers only upon such payment.

6. Liability. Designer warrants that the Deliverables will be original work and will not knowingly infringe any third party's rights. Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the total project fee, except for a party's indemnification obligations for its own willful misconduct.

7. Non-Solicitation. During the project and for six (6) months afterward, neither party will solicit for employment any employee of the other party who worked on the project.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon. The parties will first try in good faith to resolve any dispute through direct discussion for thirty (30) days. If unresolved, either party may bring a claim in the state or federal courts located in Multnomah County, Oregon, or in small claims court where eligible.

9. Entire Agreement. This Agreement and the Proposal are the entire agreement between the parties. Any change must be in writing and signed by both parties.

Client: Harbor & Pine Coffee Co.
